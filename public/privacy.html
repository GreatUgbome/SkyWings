Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you book a flight or create an account with SkyWings, we collect information including your name, email address, phone number, and passport details required for travel. We also collect payment information securely via our payment processors.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm your flight bookings.
  • To communicate with you regarding flight status, updates, or cancellations.
  • To comply with aviation security regulations and government requirements.
  • To improve our services and customer experience.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption and secure server infrastructure (Google Firebase), to protect your personal information from unauthorized access.

4. Third-Party Services

We may share necessary data with airlines, airport authorities, and government agencies as required to fulfill your travel itinerary. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.

Contact us at support@skywings.app for privacy concerns.
